Financial Distress
Intuition instead of formulas (M&M don’t offer any).

  • Value of firm
    = Value of all-equity financed company
    + PV( Tax shield )
    – PV( cost financial distress )
  • Financial distress
    • Costs of bankruptcy and financial distress
    • Agency costs in context of financial distress

Let's write that as a simple formula. What the firm's worth equals what it would be worth with no debt at all, plus the value of the tax shield, minus the cost of financial distress.

We've already put a number on the tax shield. For distress there's no neat formula, so let's just build some intuition. There are two kinds of cost. First, the direct costs of going bust or getting close to it: lawyers, fire sales, a business that stops working properly. And second, something a bit sneakier, agency costs. Once a firm gets close to trouble, shareholders and lenders start wanting very different things. The next few slides walk through a simple example of exactly that.
